BUILD NOTES

Sunny Local AI Gallery - Clean Root Android Project

Required:
- JDK 21
- Android SDK 36
- Gradle 8.13

Privacy & Offline Invariant:
- The app NEVER uploads user photos to any external server.
- The 440M EmbeddingGemma 2 LiteRT-LM model is bundled directly inside the APK assets.
- 100% on-device AI search, semantic indexing, and cleanup intelligence.

Version History:
- V1.3.0: Semantic search, exact duplicates (SHA-256), visual similarity (dHash), blur and low-resolution heuristics.
- V1.4.0: WhatsApp Intelligence Dashboard (sent vs received classification, multi-factor likely-forwarded confidence, WhatsApp large files, and WhatsApp duplicate grouping).
- V1.5.0: Visual Quality Intelligence (multi-factor quality scoring combining sharpness, exposure, resolution, and compression; burst sequence grouping with best-shot keeper ranking; explainable quality reason tags).
- V1.5.2: LiteRT-LM JNI Native Library Resolution Fix (enforced 64-bit arm64-v8a/x86_64 ABI filters to eliminate incomplete 32-bit runtimes missing liblitertlm_jni.so, configured jniLibs.useLegacyPackaging=true, and added explicit JNI preloading).
- V1.5.3: Upgraded LiteRT-LM to 0.18.0 (com.google.ai.edge.litertlm:litertlm-android:0.18.0) which supplies the previously missing ActivationDataType class definition in DEX bytecode, resolving the NoClassDefFoundError during UniversalEmbedderOptions activation mapping.
- V2.0.0: Local Gallery AI Assistant Tab & Conversational Engine (natural-language query processing, storage triage explanation, interactive candidate preview grids, one-tap batch review with protected MediaStore deletion, and contextual suggestion chips).
- V2.5.0: Video Intelligence & Multi-Format Timeline (seamless MediaStore video scanning, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO permission handling, video duration overlays and playback indicators, large video clutter triage >50MB, WhatsApp video categorization, and multi-format timeline filter toggles [All, Photos, Videos]).
- V3.0.0: Ultimate Offline AI Gallery & Semantic Cluster Engine (Automated event & story album clustering using temporal proximity sliding windows and 256d compact embedding cosine similarity; smart thematic categorization [Documents & Captures, Events, Chat Media, Moments]; dedicated 4th Albums tab with cluster inspect grids; multi-modal & multi-attribute hybrid search filtering by date ranges, media types, and quality thresholds; conversational album exploration via Local Gallery AI Assistant).
